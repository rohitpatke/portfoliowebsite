## A typical week

Three to five site appointments a day, morning battery prep, a daily 4 PM implementation huddle, a weekly CS Engineering sync, a weekly 1:1 with my co-lead and continuous updates to the Asana fulfillment board. I coordinated the electrical contractor, two logistics vendors and the account team through the fulfillment calendar and the site-visit coordination channel.

## The hard parts

- Panels with no spare slots, landlord wiring sign-offs, GFCI and breaker trips, and Wi-Fi flapping.
- An owner who asked mid-job to move the install below the meters, completed the same day.
- Reconstructing a site's scheduling history to surface a missing installer on the invite and a pending landlord sign-off.
- Recognizing the EcoFlow E-351 "grid output limited" alert as the normal 10-minute reconnection ramp rather than an overload, and proposing that the runbook say so before anyone calls an electrician.
- Sourcing replacement STREAM Ultra AC cables when none were on the open market, and opening an RMA with EcoFlow for a defective unit.
- Adopting a new returns process with the logistics vendor (SKU-labeled shelves, 24-hour notice) and inventory system access.

## Recognition

Colleagues recognized the pace and organization in Slack in April 2026: kudos for back-to-back 12+ install weeks, and thanks for keeping the team organized and handling a day of setbacks with grace. The merit case for my May raise cited the simpler install process, the customer-first mindset that kept installs on schedule through multiple 11-hour days, and the quality of my feedback to Engineering and Product.
