## Credits owned or co-owned

- **Sustainable Sites:** SSc5 heat island reduction calculations, manufacturer documentation and site plans; LTc5 access to quality transport, with a bicycle rack and transport credit analysis.
- **Water Efficiency:** WEp1 outdoor and WEp2 indoor water use reduction, plus IAQ performance calculators.
- **Rainwater Management:** Option 2 natural land cover, with a multi-tenant analysis.
- **Commissioning:** the LEED v2009 commissioning documentation list.

The LEED checklist is dated January 15, 2021. In 2022 the work expanded into water efficiency, rainwater management and commissioning documentation, plus a TVOC device survey.
