## The lifecycle

1. Site review, onboarding sheet and installation estimates.
2. Materials for install vendors, and site visits scheduled with vendors and customers.
3. Support for technicians during installs, and follow-up with customers on any issues.
4. An insights report and programming sheet before the go-live call, then a walkthrough of the documentation with the client on the call.
5. Approved programming on every thermostat, with documentation updated.
6. Pre and post graphs, a monthly manual-changes report, and all service calls, comfort issues and tickets.

## Breadth

Thermostat survey visits across two fitness chains' clubs (spring 2024), studio installs coordinated with a third-party electrician, programming for restaurant brands, Airzone-controlled sites, and triage of about 46 unreachable thermostats for one fitness client (November 2025). I also wrote client and vendor resources such as SOPs, guides and training materials.
