summary: Designed an electric vehicle charging hub on the Mumbai–Pune Expressway that harvests and stores power from solar, wind and biogas to charge intercity buses.
problem: Electrifying the busy Mumbai–Pune bus corridor needs high-power charging midway along a highway, where grid capacity is limited and a single renewable source isn't steady enough.

decisions:
  - id: three-sources
    question: Which source should power the station?
    options: [Solar only, Solar plus wind plus biogas with storage]
    chose: Solar plus wind plus biogas with storage
    why: Solar covers the day, wind often picks up at different hours, and dispatchable biogas fills the gaps, so storage can be smaller and charging more reliable.
    bubble: Combined three sources so the buses can charge around the clock.

## Scope

- Estimating the charging demand of buses on the corridor.
- Sizing each source and the storage bank.
- Choosing a site on the expressway between the two cities.
