## Content analysis

After completing CS50P, I coded the course under four themes:

- **Ease of use:** rich formats (video, notes, slides, source code, transcripts, subtitles) and fully on-demand, but the color scheme hurts readability.
- **Engagement:** section-wise materials, Q&A, Zoom sessions and Puzzle Day, but no "resume where you left off" and English only.
- **Efficacy:** practical skills that carry into automation and AI/ML work.
- **Finances:** completely free, with optional paid certification.

## Recommendations

1. Add multilingual support.
2. Redesign the color scheme and add progress saving.
3. Update content on a regular schedule (last refreshed in 2022).
4. Add light gamification such as badges for finished sections.
5. Survey learners to guide the next revision.

The capstone artifact carried this into wireframes and a prototype addressing the usability gaps.
