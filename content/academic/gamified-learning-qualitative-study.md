## Study design

- **Research questions:** how Duolingo and Kahoot! shape motivation, engagement, affect and perceived learning; whether gamification feels engaging or anxiety-inducing; which elements students enjoy most.
- **Framework:** Self-Determination Theory and Flow Theory.
- **Recruitment:** platform forums, Reddit, peers and the school newsletter; interviews over Zoom, Meet or Teams.
- **Analysis:** transcription, coding, memoing, thematic analysis, member checking and an audit trail.
- **Ethics:** informed consent, de-identification, deletion of recordings, IRB review and the right to skip or stop.

## Critical research review

I reviewed Fisher and Hitchcock (2022), *Enhancing Student Learning and Engagement Using Digital Stories*, against a rubric. It was strong on fit between method and question and on anonymity, and weak on sample size and triangulation. I then rewrote it as a plain-language blog post. About 85% of students preferred the digital story to a paper.
