## Coverage

- **Collection:** profile information, device and location, cookies and service use.
- **Use:** services, Premium, communications, advertising, marketing, and product research.
- **Sharing:** affiliates, service providers, legal requests, a sale or merger, and public profile visibility to search engines.
- **Member choices:** access, delete or correct data, control visibility, manage ad preferences, close the account (some data is retained).
- **Security and transfers:** safeguards with no guarantee against breaches, and processing outside the member's country.
- **Lawful bases and rights:** consent, contract and legitimate interest, plus extra rights for EU, EEA and Swiss residents.
