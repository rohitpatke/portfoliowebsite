## Khanmigo case study

I assessed Khan Academy's AI tutor on privacy, fairness and transparency, then proposed a framework: data minimization with auto-deletion of sensitive prompts, student-friendly explanations and teacher access to prompt logs, routine bias monitoring, multilingual and accessible content, and parent dashboards for retention and permissions.

- **Personalized learning:** how adaptive systems tailor pace and content.
