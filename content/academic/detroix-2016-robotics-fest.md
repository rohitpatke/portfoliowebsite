## What I handled

- **Events:** Robo Soccer, Robo Race, Blind Race, Mystery Maze, Brain Bot, Sumo War, Line Follower, Mount the Circuit, Laser Tag, Web Master, Tech Quiz and more.
- **Budget and requirements:** costing for bots, batteries, arenas and prizes for each event, plus a revenue sheet that set entry fees against prizes.
- **Schedule and website:** the event calendar and the fest website.
