title: Exercise and stress in working adults, a publication plan
summary: With three classmates, framed a study on how exercise reduces stress among working professionals and planned how to publish it to both academic and professional audiences.
problem: Many working adults skip exercise and pay for it in long-term health, yet the research that could persuade them sits in journals they never read.
timeline: 2025-05 to 2025-07
skills: [Academic Writing, Research Dissemination, Scientific Communication, Presentation, Teamwork]

venue: EDD795 Writing for Scholarly Publication, Judson University
authors: [Twinkle Mohan, Rohit Patke, Dimple Bhanushali, Shraddha Patel]
role: Group member

decisions:
  - id: two-audiences
    question: Where should the findings go?
    options: [A single peer-reviewed journal, Journals plus LinkedIn and an interactive platform]
    chose: Journals plus LinkedIn and an interactive platform
    why: Working professionals are the people who need the result. We paired journal targets (Journal of Physical Activity & Health, SportRxiv) with a LinkedIn campaign and an interactive results page.

## Deliverables

- A problem and solution framing for the study.
- A marketing and dissemination proposal naming target journals, preprint servers and professional channels.
